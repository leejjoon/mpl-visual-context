==============
 User's Guide
==============

Welcome to the ``mpl-visual-context`` User Guide! This guide provides detailed explanations, examples, and best practices to help you leverage the full potential of the library and enhance your Matplotlib visualizations.

Whether you're looking to apply complex visual effects, add contextual information to your plots, or streamline your plotting workflow, these guides are designed to walk you through the key features and functionalities. As the library grows, you'll find more guides here covering its various aspects.
